About Einstein

Albert Einstein is one of the worlds most famous scientists. He is famous for his contributions to science as a whole, having written the equation E=MC squared to represent mass energy equivalence, among other acievements like winning a nobel prize

The Universe and Dr.Einstein is a book not written by Einstein that breaks down some of his scientific theories for readers, while Einstein on Peace is a long collection containing many of Einstein's letters and writings pertaining to peace and politics
